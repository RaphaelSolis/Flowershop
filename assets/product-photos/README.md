# Complete catalog photographs

Catalog thumbnails are made from the original photo-only PNG artwork in the Collections, Occasions, and Customize project folders. Each original is trimmed to its transparent bounds, fitted within 672 × 672 pixels without changing its proportions, then centered on a transparent 720 × 720 WebP canvas. The 24-pixel minimum margin preserves all flowers, wrapping, stems, baskets, pots, and gifts. No labels or prices are embedded in these photographs.

Collections use the `flower`, `bou flo`, `new flo`, `best flo`, `pot flo`, and `gift flo` originals. Occasions use `birth flo`, `wedd flo` (or `wed flo 4`), `anniv flo`, `ach flo`, `sym flo`, and `ty flo`. Customize uses `cost flo` and `add flo`.

The catalog and cart display these images with `object-fit: contain`. Captions and prices remain live text. Transparent margins avoid the colored bars and truncated edges in the earlier thumbnails.
